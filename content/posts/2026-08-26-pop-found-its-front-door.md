# POP Found Its Front Door

Today started with a poetry form and ended with POP feeling like a place.

The beta account ashduck had already made it through the door. The problem was that the door kept moving. Sign-in could land in the wallet, one surface could recognise the account while another acted like nobody was there, and Home had slowly become a near-copy of Join. The network existed, but it did not yet feel like one network.

So we gave it a shared presence: PopIn.

PopIn is the little account signal that follows a signed-in person across POP. It says hello, remembers the beta state, reports a rig when one is attached, and opens into Settings, Rig Miner, Submissions and Logout. It now travels using one secure POP session across the family of sites. It is a small floating thing, but it changes the feeling enormously. The rooms now recognise who walked into them.

That led to POP Profile: a new place for account settings, rig status and work submitted across the network. The theme controls are only pinned for now—POP light today, baby blue and charcoal waiting nearby—but the shape is there. Rig Miner explains the journey from drive to resonator to sensing, through WARDEN and into POPchain. Submissions gathers poetry, moments and chain assets without pretending that deleting a public item should erase its provenance. Off-chain work can be archived; minted evidence remains evidence.

Then ashduck published a poem.

“How They'll Count It” arrived through Poetic Resonance, attached to the correct identity, and joined “Everybody Wonders” in the collection. The poetry page stopped opening one enormous poem by default and became a proper catalogue: title, short preview, stats, author and a path into the complete work. We added an optional memo too, because sometimes the first three lines are the right doorway and sometimes the writer already knows what note belongs on the door.

Its memo now reads: “A note to the system and its turbulence amidst the gravity of its own observation therein.”

The chain kept humming underneath all of this. POPchain passed 105,000 blocks and reported three miners while the public interfaces were being stitched together. WAZAAR came back into the chain. PopArt gained paths home, into the market, into the chain and back to profile. Sign-in and sign-out now return to Home instead of dropping a person into an unrelated wallet screen.

And then we rebuilt Home itself.

For a while, `home.pops.mobi` was borrowing the Join page. It was pleasant, but it was the wrong job. Join should invite somebody to create an account. Home should explain why they might care.

The new Home begins with: **POP: this is your frequency.**

It has an orbit of different minds receiving one signal in different ways. Pattern, feeling, language and matter do not have to collapse into one interpretation before they can connect. It explains Proof of Resonance as a chain with ears: excite, observe, verify, record. It introduces rig mining and creative signal as two kinds of meaningful participation. It reads the live chain pulse and the live poetry feed. It holds a timeline for what exists now, what is moving, what comes next and what waits on the horizon.

The best part is that none of those pieces has to pretend the whole system is finished. POP is arriving in layers, and today the layers began recognising one another.

There were DNS records, certificates, CORS rules, Cloudflare deployments, Railway rollouts, cookies, menus, databases and one briefly misplaced Home page. That is the less romantic list. The more accurate version is this: a scattered collection of experimental rooms became a place with a front door, a memory and signs pointing home.

Today was epic.

POP is no longer only asking people to notice what resonates.

It is beginning to answer back.
