Today we gave the system a cleaner shape. WEIHR now has its own global `weihr` command for identities, policies, roles, certificates, registry data, and classes.

The original `pop weihr` path still works as a compatibility alias, so nothing had to be left behind. Dux:Ops, POP, WEIHR, and Warden are beginning to stand in their own places—and it feels really good to see the architecture becoming clearer.
