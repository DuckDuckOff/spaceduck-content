# POP: This Is Your Frequency

Today we made the front door feel like a front door again.

The work was small in pixels and large in consequence. On Home, the **Enter POP** action had started sending people away to the Join surface. That made the first step feel like a detour, especially for people who already had an account. We brought it back into the room: Enter POP now opens the shared PopIn sign-in module over the Home page, keeping the person, the context and the invitation together.

PopIn is becoming one of the quiet foundations of POP. It is the little persistent signal that says who is present, carries beta status and rig context, and gives the account a way to move through Home, PopArt, the chain, WAZAAR and Profile without becoming a stranger at every doorway. The interface stays light and calm. Underneath it, the session boundary is doing serious work.

We also clarified the way a crystal enters the system.

The WEIHR landing page no longer puts frequency, phase and Q fields directly in front of an anonymous visitor. Instead, it now begins with a governed **Register a crystal** path at [`weihr.org/register`](https://weihr.org/register/). That page explains the POP Proof of Resonance model and the responsibilities around it: attributable identity, transparent infrastructure documentation, approved disclosure terms, and WARDEN review before a profile becomes trusted evidence or supports privileged action.

The actual parameter submission remains behind the existing signed-in POPchain application flow. A person can join POP first, or continue with the POP account they already have. The form is still there when it is appropriate; it is simply no longer pretending that a few numbers typed into a public landing page are the same thing as verified resonance.

That distinction is important to the idea itself. Proof of Resonance is not only frequency, phase and Q. It is measurement with context, identity with boundaries, and evidence that can be inspected without being inflated into a claim it cannot support. WEIHR provides the policy and identity layer. WARDEN checks fresh physical evidence. POPchain records what has actually been accepted.

Home now says the idea more clearly: **POP: this is your frequency.** A poem, a pattern, a sound, a market, or a resonant machine can become a signal. Different minds can transform the same signal without needing to flatten one another first. That is the creative and physical network we are trying to build—a place where attention becomes expression, expression gains provenance, and meaningful overlap can become something shared.

The practical work behind the scenes was the usual strange mixture: a static-page deployment, a production promotion, a small event-hook bug, a careful verification against the live custom domain, and a commit that preserved the work without absorbing unrelated workspace changes. The first deployment reached preview correctly; the second was promoted to production so Home itself received the repair. That final check mattered. A beautiful preview is not the same thing as a working front door.

There is still plenty to build: richer account choices inside PopIn, deeper crystal evidence, collection economics, and the Qube path from ordinary physical objects into repeatable resonance work. But the direction is holding.

The invitation is now simple again:

Come in.

Sign in if you are already here. Join if you are new. Notice what resonates.
