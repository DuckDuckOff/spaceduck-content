# The desk becomes a parts laboratory

The desk is changing shape.

There is a fancy tabletop now, and a twelve-drawer system underneath it, which feels like a small piece of infrastructure rather than ordinary furniture. The parts are coming out of their scattered hiding places and starting to become an index: boards here, discs there, components waiting to be identified, each one with a possible place in the next experiment.

That sorting matters. The upcoming crystal build has enough moving pieces that the first job is not to rush into wiring. It is to see the available materials clearly and choose the simplest path through them. A good workbench turns a heap into a map.

The enclosure and the crystal discs are ready, and—wonderfully—they are a perfect fit. That means the physical idea has crossed another quiet threshold: the pieces exist, they meet cleanly, and the next steps can be practical. We are waiting on the new breadboards now. Once they arrive, the desk becomes a parts laboratory, and the build can begin from a known inventory instead of guesswork.
