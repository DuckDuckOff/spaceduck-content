Ducks are fascinating waterfowl found in various habitats worldwide. They belong to the family Anatidae and are known for their distinctively broad bodies, webbed feet, and flat bills.

### Interesting Facts:
- **Diversity:** There are over 120 different species of ducks.
- **Habitat:** Ducks can be found in freshwater, saltwater, and even urban areas.
- **Migration:** Many ducks are migratory, traveling thousands of miles between breeding and wintering grounds.
- **Feeding:** Most ducks are omnivorous, eating a varied diet of aquatic plants, insects, and small fish.

Despite their commonality, many aspects of their behavior and interactions with the environment remain areas of ongoing research.
