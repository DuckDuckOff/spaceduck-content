# A Temporary Pin in the Crystal Path

Today was a long systems day: part integration, part troubleshooting, and part
returning to the physical question at the centre of POP—how do we let a crystal
become a real, measurable participant in the chain?

We spent time on R1 compatibility and the surrounding computer-to-agent path,
with the practical goal of making more of the POPChain accessible through the
same small device. That work is still settling, but the direction is clearer:
the device should be a useful doorway into the wider system, not an isolated
demo.

On the hardware side, we put a temporary pin in the actual crystal path. That
is not a retreat; it is a deliberate pause while the next measurement layer is
made more reliable. We looked closely at the ADC path and the resonance
generator module, separating the questions of driving, sensing, conditioning,
and recording. The important result was a plan for the next pass: establish a
clean baseline, measure the response across the expected resonance range, and
only then commit to the more permanent crystal interface.

The hardware does not need to be rushed into a final shape. A good instrument
starts by making the uncertain parts observable. The ADC gives us a way to
listen. The resonance generator gives us a controlled way to ask a question.
Together they should let the crystal’s behaviour become data we can understand,
compare, and eventually connect back into POPChain identity.

We also put a funding plan in place. The next step is to turn the work already
done into a concise application package: the problem and opportunity, the
technical approach, the current prototype evidence, a small set of measurable
milestones, a realistic budget, and a clear account of what the funding would
unlock. We will identify suitable programs, prepare the supporting material,
and begin applying soon, with the immediate focus on the sensing path,
prototype refinement, and the POPChain integration that makes the physical
work useful beyond the bench.

For now, the crystal path has a temporary pin in it, the ADC and resonance
generator have a clearer job, and the project has a practical route toward its
next stage. Sometimes progress is not another permanent connection. Sometimes
it is knowing exactly where to pause so the next connection can be trusted.
