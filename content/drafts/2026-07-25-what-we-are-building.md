# What We Are Building

This project is an attempt to build a small, physical, human-scale network from the ground up.

At the centre is a device: breadboards, sensors, a resonant crystal system, an enclosure, and the practical hardware needed to make the signal measurable in the real world. The point is not to hide everything behind a screen. The point is to make something that can be held, tested, repaired, and understood.

POPchain is the network underneath it. It records proof-of-resonance activity and turns measured participation into a shared chain of state and rewards.

WEIHR is the identity and trust layer. It is where resonant identities, permissions, policies, and administrative authority are made explicit instead of being left as invisible assumptions.

SynapSes is the system for turning meaningful opportunities into structured, living instances. It gives each project or node a boundary, memory, and a path from recognition to something that can actually be built.

Spaceduck is the public journal around all of it: the place for field notes, honest progress, ordinary life, failures, decisions, and the moments that would otherwise disappear.

So the whole thing is part instrument, part network, part operating system, and part record of becoming. It is still early. Some pieces are simulated, some are live, and some are still waiting in shipping boxes. But the direction is clear: build systems that connect physical reality, accountable identity, and shared memory without pretending the unfinished parts are already solved.
